Manual vs automatic rules

Manual: either a bigger or smaller ad may be above the other. There are no size-order, pairing, 260 CC or full-page-exception restrictions on manual placement. Physical page bounds, no overlaps, whole-column/cm snapping, and no floating always apply.

Your grab point is preserved when moving an already placed ad. The preview follows both horizontal and vertical movement. Unsupported drops settle downward. Colliding ads are pushed upward when space permits; every resulting placement must have full bottom support. Red preview means that drop cannot be accepted.

No floating means the entire bottom edge is supported by the page bottom or ads beneath it. Moving/removing a supporting ad first tries to keep other ads where they are; if needed they settle vertically with their column positions and order preserved. If that cannot produce full support, the operation is refused, not silently reordered widest-first. Move upper ads first if necessary.

Automatic: defaults to 260 CC per normal page; at most two exception pages may use 384 CC (50 cm single-page ads use up to 400 CC on an exceptional 50 cm page). It processes wider ads first, tries your listed pairs, and does not add a wider ad above narrower supporting ads. Two-ad pairs can differ in height; equal heights are preferred. The 3+2+1 triple requires equal heights. Several greedy strategies are compared; this is not an exhaustive optimal packing solver.

“Place remaining” preserves existing manual work and skips over-cap pages. “Rebuild” deliberately replaces the layout with automatic ordering, after confirmation. Undo can restore the previous arrangement.

The usable model is exactly 8 columns × 48 cm (32 × 48 cm). Margins/gutters are not subtracted. Print output is a reduced A3 planning preview, not press artwork. Work is saved in this browser; JSON backups are portable. Optional Jev uses the included local server. Offline planning makes no API requests. No external JavaScript libraries.

Manual freedom: bigger or smaller ads can go above or below each other. Drag controls both directions. Every ad needs full bottom support. Collisions can push ads upward; affected ads then settle downward.
